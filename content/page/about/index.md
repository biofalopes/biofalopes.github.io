## How I work

My work bridges different areas of technology, from networking, operating systems, and core infrastructure to software architecture, containers, and CI/CD pipelines. I focus on simplifying operations, reducing manual work, and building solutions that remain reliable, maintainable, and sustainable over time.

I also have experience providing technical leadership on complex projects, collaborating with development, infrastructure, and security teams, external partners, and management to turn business requirements into practical, well-designed technical solutions.

I'm fluent in English and interested in international opportunities, particularly **Senior/Staff Platform Engineer, Cloud Infrastructure Engineer, and Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)** roles, including remote B2B contracts.

## About this blog

This blog is where I share hands-on experiences, technical challenges, and lessons learned from my day-to-day work, mainly around cloud computing, infrastructure, automation, Kubernetes, solution architecture, and platform engineering.

My goal is to discuss real-world scenarios, explain the reasoning behind technical decisions, and explore different approaches while acknowledging the context, constraints, and trade-offs involved.

Rather than presenting one-size-fits-all solutions, I aim to share what I've learned from working with complex environments, including what worked, what didn't, and what I would approach differently.
